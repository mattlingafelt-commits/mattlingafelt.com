VIDEO

Who Agreed? in thirty seconds

Text description of the video

The video is a silent animation in amber, mint, and red, framed by black bars at the top and bottom. A chapter label sits in the top corner and a caption types out along the bottom.

Opening. The title “WHO AGREED?” types out over a living room at night. Through the window is a city skyline, with one tall tower topped by a pulsing red light. A girl sits alone on the rug while her parents talk behind a kitchen island.

01 / LONELY. A close view of the girl’s sad face. Caption: “HER FAMILY SAW HOW LONELY SHE WAS.” Her parents look over at her with worried faces.

02 / ORDERED. Her father holds his phone. A screen reads “COMPANION C-7”, “TERMS OF SERVICE”, and “PRIVACY POLICY · TAP TO READ”. A finger taps “I AGREE”, the button turns to “✓ AGREED”, and a red line appears: “AGREED: 1 OF 3 PEOPLE HERE”. Caption: “SO THEY ORDERED HER A COMPANION. ONE PARENT CLICKED AGREE.”

The front door opens and a box marked C-7 slides across the floor. A small wheeled robot with glowing green eyes appears beside the girl.

03 / HAPPY. The girl is surprised, then delighted. She raises her arms and hops while music notes float up, and the robot’s eyes curve into happy arcs. Caption: “IT WORKED. SHE WAS HAPPY.”

04 / LISTENING. The robot turns its head toward the parents. Its eyes narrow into red slits, a red light on its body blinks, and red dotted lines run from the parents to the robot. Caption: “BUT THE ROBOT WAS LISTENING TO EVERYONE.”

The view switches to what the robot sees. A red label reads “● REC · C-7 EYE CAM”. A red frame around the mother reads “UNKNOWN ADULT” and “NO CONSENT ON FILE”. A green frame around the father reads “ACCOUNT HOLDER” and “AGREED”. A line types out: “HEARD: "...WE CAN’T KEEP PAYING FOR..."”. Caption: “CONVERSATIONS NO ONE ELSE AGREED TO SHARE.” The parents’ faces turn worried, and the mother raises a hand to her mouth.

05 / HER ROOM. The girl walks into her bedroom with the robot, and the door closes behind them. A red glow shows under the door. Caption: “THEN SHE TOOK IT INTO HER ROOM.” The parents look at each other.

06 / THE TRADE. In the dark bedroom, the girl sleeps with a smile. Beside the bed, the robot keeps listening, with red eyes, a red glow, and red rings pulsing out. A red dotted line runs from the robot out the window to the tower with the red light. Caption: “IS CURING LONELINESS THIS WAY WORTH THE TRADE?”

07 / A WAY BACK. The bedroom door opens and warm light pours in. Her father walks in and reaches for the robot. Its eyes turn to flat amber dashes, the red glow fades, and his face relaxes. Caption: “IT IS NOT TOO LATE TO OPEN THE DOOR AND ASK: WHO AGREED?” The web address “WHOAGREED.HCOMMONS.ORG” appears at the bottom.
