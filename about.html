Who I am

I am a theologian who studies robots, a former teacher who still thinks in lesson plans, and a writer who believes ethics belongs in township meetings as much as seminar rooms.

You can find me at Shared Theology and on the Dead Theologians Podcast, writing and talking about what happens when technology moves into our homes and communities.

Experience

Education
PhD, Public Theology and Ethics, United Lutheran Seminary. In progress.
MA, Theological Studies, Pittsburgh Theological Seminary, 2014.
BA, Theological Studies, University of Valley Forge, 2010. Summa cum laude.
Teaching and ministry
Hilton Head Christian Academy, 2021 to 2026. Department Chair. Taught Bible, ethics, and research courses in grades 6 through 12.
Community Worship Center, January 2020. Small Groups and Discipleship Pastor.
Technology and AI
Technology and AI implementation for a coaching and consulting firm serving owners of contracting and trades businesses. Most of that work is AI governance, making sure an organization's AI use policy actually gets followed and doesn't just sit in a document.
Pursuing the IAPP Artificial Intelligence Governance Professional (AIGP) certification.
