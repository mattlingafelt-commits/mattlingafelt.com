Privacy

I study what technology collects about the people around it, so this site collects as little as I can manage.

What this site doesn’t do

This site doesn’t use cookies, analytics, ads, trackers, or sign-ups. There are no forms. Every page, font, and image loads from this site itself, so no other company sees that you visited.

What the host sees

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles basic technical information to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks, such as your IP address, your browser type, and which pages were requested. I don’t use Cloudflare’s analytics, and I don’t receive or look at that information. You can read Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

If you email me

I’ll use your address and your message only to reply to you. I won’t add you to a list or share your email with anyone.

Links to other sites

Some links go to other sites, like Substack, Spotify, Knowledge Commons, ORCID, LinkedIn, and the publications that carry my writing. Once you follow one, that site’s own privacy policy applies.

Questions

If you have a question about this page, email me at MattLingafelt@gmail.com. If anything about how this site works changes, I’ll update this page and the date at the top.
