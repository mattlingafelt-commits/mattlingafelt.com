Reflection

IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

Here I look back on the semester, at how my writing for the public changed and what I learned about the people I'm writing for.

Since my first post, my perspective has opened up about who is encompassed by the terms “direct” and “indirect stakeholders.” I also moved from writing very personally to letting more of the academic in me come out. A blog shouldn’t be heavy with terminology, but a piece can be informed and still mindful of its audience. I hope I’ve struck that balance.

I’ve learned that there isn’t a set, shared vocabulary in the space I’m writing in, and that the issue is more alive than I expected, with polarizing views about AI that carry over to the technology that houses it.

This archive still tells the companies’ stories instead of highlighting user experiences. That came from one decision: I made the documents the items, so even “From the room” is my reading, not the families’. I hope to build their voices into the site.
