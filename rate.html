Rate the robots

How comfortable would you be?

Here are the ten companion robots from my archive, Who Agreed? Rate how comfortable you’d be having each one in your home, from 1 to 10, and tell me why in one to three sentences. Rate as many or as few as you like.

What I collect, and what I don’t

What I collect

  • The rating you pick, 1 to 10, for each robot you rate
  • Your reason, one to three sentences, if you write one
  • The date you sent it

What I don’t collect

  • Your name, email, or any account
  • Cookies or trackers of any kind
  • Your IP address. It isn’t saved with your answer.

What happens to it

  • Each robot’s average shows on this page for everyone
  • I read every reason before any of it appears here
  • I may quote reasons on this site, without names

More detail is on the privacy page.

CBA-01 · Enabot

Enabot EBO

A camera on wheels that the owner drives around the house from somewhere else, to check on family and pets.

Senses
A 4K camera, four microphones, a distance sensor for obstacles, and infrared for docking. It maps the house with its camera.
Collects
Live video and audio for the owner watching from somewhere else.
Processed
Streams over Wi-Fi to the owner’s app. Its voice assistant runs on GPT-4o mini, a cloud AI model.
