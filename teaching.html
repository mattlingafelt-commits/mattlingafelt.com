Teaching philosophy

“If an argument only holds together in the vocabulary of the seminar, it is not finished.

I teach contested material by putting students inside the argument rather than around it. The aim was never to settle the question inside a class period. It was to make students competent in disagreement, which is a skill they keep long after they forget the particulars.

¶ Featured practice

The structured debate in Old Testament Ethics

I built Old Testament Ethics around a structured debate format in which students had to argue a position they did not hold and then answer the strongest version of the objection.

In 2022 I presented the model at the League of Christian Schools Conference: “Teaching Old Testament Ethics in the High School Curriculum,” a debate-based model for teaching Old Testament ethics with an evaluation of supporting resources.

Curriculum and ministry

A K-12 sequence

Designed and developed a sequential K-12 curriculum, restructuring the course sequence so each grade builds on the last. I plan backward from the capacity I want a graduating student to have rather than forward from the material I want to cover.

Through the arrival of AI

Taught grades 6 to 12 through the arrival of generative AI in the classroom. Tools should support rather than supplant the judgment a student is there to develop, and I would rather read something rough that is genuinely a student’s own than something polished that is not.

Small groups, 2020

As Small Groups and Discipleship Pastor at Community Worship Center, I researched and developed small group curriculum on the Kingdom of God and Kingdom of Heaven in their original context, then lectured and directed discussion on its application for contemporary American audiences.

Beyond the classroom Planned and directed a high school retreat for more than 300 students and staff.

Now a student again

Doctoral coursework, United Lutheran Seminary.

HT 901

PhD Seminar in Public Theology 1

The program’s foundational seminar, working through what public theology is as a discipline and what it owes to audiences outside both the church and the academy. It is where I am developing Value Sensitive Design as the design-ethics method for my own project.

HT 930

Digital and Public Humanities

Digital humanities method and its public application, working across Omeka, Voyant, VADER, Gephi, TEI encoding, TimelineJS, and video. The question underneath the tools is how humanities scholarship reaches people who will never open the journal article.

ST 734/934

Political Theology

Theology’s relationship to political order and power, read through Schmitt, Metz, and the tradition that follows them.
