About me

I am a Christian ethicist, PhD student, and former teacher who studies robots and the families who bring them home.

You can find me at Shared Theology and on the Dead Theologians Podcast, writing and talking about what happens when technology moves into our homes and communities.

Technology and AI

AI and Tech Stack Operator since June 2026, leading technology and AI implementation for a coaching and consulting firm serving owners of contracting and trades businesses. One of my projects there is AI governance, making sure our AI use policy actually gets followed and doesn’t just sit in a document.

Teaching and ministry

Hilton Head Christian Academy, 2021 to 2026. Department Chair. Taught Bible, Theology, Christian Ethics, Research, and Psychology in grades 6 through 12.

Community Worship Center, January 2020. Small Groups and Discipleship Pastor.
